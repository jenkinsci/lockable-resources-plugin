Global resource groups have a name and an optional description. Assign resources to a group on the Lockable Resources page and select it with lock(group: 'plc', label: 'S7'). Resources without a group belong to common; requests without a group only use common.

Groups referenced by resources are included automatically. Removing a group entry does not move or delete its resources. Empty groups can be configured here before adding resources.

Groups are global selection boundaries, not job permissions or folder-scoped resources.
